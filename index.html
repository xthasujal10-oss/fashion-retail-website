What we build on

Three things we won't compromise on, season after season.

Natural fibres

Wool, linen, cotton and alpaca — materials that breathe, age well, and break down at the end of their life.

Made to last

French seams, reinforced stress points, and buttons we can replace by hand rather than a whole garment.

Small batches

We cut in runs of under 200 pieces, so nothing sits in a warehouse waiting to be discounted.

Hear about new seasons first

One or two emails a month — new arrivals, restocks, and the occasional care guide. No spam.
