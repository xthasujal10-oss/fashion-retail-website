Our story

Started with one coat that wouldn't wear out.

Hearth & Hem began in 2019 with a single wool coat, cut for a friend who wanted something she'd still own in ten years. That coat is still in her wardrobe. Everything we've made since has been held to the same standard.

We're a team of six, working with three family-run mills in Yorkshire, Ghent and Prato. We visit each of them at least twice a year, and we know the people running the looms by name.

How a garment gets made

Four steps, roughly six months, from raw fibre to a finished piece.

  1. Source

    We choose fibre first — traceable wool, organic cotton, European linen — before we design anything around it.

  2. Pattern

    Every pattern is tested across three fit sessions with people outside our team before it's approved.

  3. Make

    Small runs are cut and sewn by the same two workshops we've used since 2020.

  4. Finish

    Each piece is steamed, inspected by hand, and paired with a care card before it ships.
